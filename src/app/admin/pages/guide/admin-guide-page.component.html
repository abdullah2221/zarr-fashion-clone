Admin Guide

How to run your shop

Step-by-step instructions for everyday tasks. No technical knowledge is needed. Every screen also carries its own short hints — this guide is the full version in one place.

1. Change the homepage

  1. Open Pages and select the Home page on the left.
  2. Its sections are listed in the order visitors see them, top to bottom.
  3. Click a section to edit it. The form tells you in plain words what that section shows.
  4. To move a section, change its Order on the page: lower numbers show first, 0 is the very top.
  5. To remove a section from the site without deleting it, untick Section is visible and save.
  6. Some sections get their content from other screens — the form tells you exactly where, e.g. "Admin > Campaigns".

2. Add a product

  1. Open Products and choose New product.
  2. Fill in the name, prices, category and stock. The web address is filled in automatically from the name.
  3. Add photos: upload a new photo, or pick one already in the shop with Choose from library.
  4. Tick Publish product to show it in the shop. Untick it to keep it as a draft.
  5. Tick Feature in storefront only for products you want highlighted.
  6. Save, then open the shop in a new tab and check the product looks right.

3. Make a banner or campaign card

  1. Open Campaigns.
  2. Hero banners are the big sliding images at the top of the homepage. Every active banner appears there, in order.
  3. Campaigns are the smaller cards and strips. When you make one, choose Where does it show? — that is the only place it will appear.
  4. Set a start and end date if the campaign should stop on its own, e.g. after Eid.
  5. Untick Active to pause a banner or campaign without deleting it.

4. Change the menus

  1. Open Settings and find Navigation menus.
  2. Each menu is a list of rows: a label (what visitors see) and a link (where it goes).
  3. Use Up and Down to reorder, Add link for a new row, and Add sub-link for a dropdown item under it.
  4. A sub-link shows as a dropdown on computers and as an expandable group on phones.
  5. Footer columns are still typed as text: a line starting with # is a column title, then one Label | /link line per link.

5. Handle an order

  1. Open Orders and click an order to open it.
  2. Change its status as the order moves (for example, from pending to packed to delivered) and add a short note if you like.
  3. Every status change is recorded with your note, so you can always see the order's history.
  4. Customer details live in Customers. Do not delete orders to tidy up — cancelled orders are part of your records.

6. Use photos safely

  1. Open Media to see every uploaded image in the shop.
  2. When a form offers Choose from library, picking a photo reuses the same file — it is not copied.
  3. Deleting a product, banner or campaign never deletes a photo that another item still uses.
  4. For product photos, use a clear photo of the real product. A photo of a different item confuses customers.

7. What "hidden" means

Nothing is half-shown on the site. The rules are simple:

  • Hidden sections are skipped completely — visitors see no gap where they were.
  • Empty sections hide themselves. If a section's content is empty (no active testimonials, no active campaign tiles), the section does not appear at all.
  • Inactive products and categories do not appear in the shop until you publish them.
  • If something disappeared from the site, check Visible / Active / Publish on that item first — that is the usual cause.

8. Words explained

Page address
The web address part of a page or product, e.g. "about" for /about. Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes only. It is locked after creation so old links keep working.
Order on the page
The number that decides the order of sections, banners or categories. Lower numbers show first.
Visible / Active / Publish
Three words for the same idea: this item shows on the site. Untick any of them to hide it without deleting it.
Where does it show?
For campaigns, the one place on the site where the card appears — for example, the homepage or the offers grid.
SKU
Your own stock code for a product, e.g. LN-101. Customers never see it.
SEO title / description
The title and short description search engines show for a page or product. If left empty, the shop's default is used.
Alt text
A short description of an image, read aloud for visitors who cannot see it. One plain sentence is enough.

Safe habit: make one change, save, then open the shop in a new tab and check it. If anything looks wrong, the change you just made is the first place to look.
