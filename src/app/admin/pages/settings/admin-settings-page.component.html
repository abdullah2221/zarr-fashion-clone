Navigation menus

Header and mobile menus are edited as rows: label, URL, and the active (current-page link in the header) and highlight (campaign colour) flags. Reorder with Up/Down. A sub-link under a header or mobile link renders as a dropdown on desktop and an expandable group on mobile. Footer columns are still edited as text: a # Column title line, followed by Label | URL lines.
