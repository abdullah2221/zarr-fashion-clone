Pages & Sections

Every page is an ordered list of section blocks, shown top to bottom in the order listed below. Reorder sections with the sort order, hide them with the visibility toggle, and edit each section's own content fields. Card content (products, categories, campaigns, banners) keeps its own admin screens — each section below says where its content lives. Hidden sections are skipped completely on the live site: visitors see no gap where they were.
